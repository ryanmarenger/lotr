# Site Card → Landscape Mapping

229 site cards → 46 base cinemagraph landscapes.
Each landscape is one animated background. Multiple site cards share a landscape.
Mood variants (time of day, weather, corruption) are CSS overlay effects on the base image.

## MOOD LAYERS (CSS overlays on any base landscape)

### Time of Day
- **Dawn**: warm orange tint, low contrast, soft shadows
- **Day**: neutral, full brightness
- **Dusk**: deep orange/purple, long shadows
- **Night**: deep blue, high contrast, moonlight

### Weather
- **Clear**: no overlay
- **Rain**: desaturation, blue shift, rain particle overlay
- **Fog**: white vignette, reduced visibility
- **Storm**: dark, lightning flash, heavy desaturation
- **Snow**: white overlay, cold blue tint

### Corruption (universal, burden-driven)
- **0-3 burdens**: no effect
- **4-6**: subtle desaturation, faint red tint at edges
- **7-9**: noticeable red vignette, landscape darkens
- **10-11**: heavy red/black, landscape nearly consumed
- **12**: near-total darkness, only the Eye remains

All mood layers are CSS `filter` + `mix-blend-mode` overlays. Zero additional images needed.
